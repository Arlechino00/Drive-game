## Design decisions

- Cruise speed in km/h: 130 + 200*(1-exp(-minutes/20)) + 1.4*minutes. Minutes primarily use driving time, with a small distance contribution. No gameplay speed cap; braking/damage still override actual target, but cannot freeze progression.
- Look-ahead scales with current speed, with a spatial budget sized beyond tested 400 km/h. Row spacing scales with closing speed and vehicle footprints to retain enough time for an adjacent-lane maneuver. Road/actor capacity is fixed and inspectable.
- Mixed-speed/overtaking traffic uses controlled, sparse single-actor rows with a reserved outer corridor; paired/static rows retain the existing safe-lane chain and drain semantics. Never vary individual speeds inside paired rows.
- Heat stays in the run, radar adds severity/chain pressure, and escaping lowers it gradually. Radar never awards points for speeding or gives instant unavoidable arrest.
- Police contact contributes pressure; only sustained capture reaches arrest. Escape requires actual separation and disengagement, with Boost optional.
- Localization migrates missing/invalid language to English and leaves scores/access/settings intact. All visible text belongs in keyed resources, with live UI refresh.
